A help center rarely breaks all at once. It drifts: a page is copied, a policy changes, a product is renamed. This checklist covers the ten checks that catch most of the drift.

In short

  • Start with contradictions and outdated pages. They cause wrong answers.
  • Then clean up duplicates, broken links, thin pages and orphans. They add noise.
  • Repeat on a schedule. A one-off audit goes stale within months.

The checklist

  1. Contradictions

    Compare the prices, limits, deadlines and steps that different pages state about the same thing. Fix the wrong page, or better, replace the repeated number with a link to one authoritative page.

  2. Duplicate pages

    Look for two pages with nearly the same text. Keep the better one, redirect the other to it, and update links.

  3. Repeated sections

    The same paragraph pasted into many articles will be edited in some places and not others. Move it into one page and link to it, or use a shared snippet in your docs tool.

  4. Outdated pages

    Search for old years, "deprecated", "legacy" and old version numbers. Update the page, label it as legacy, or remove it.

  5. Broken links

    Check internal and external links. Replace or remove links that return 404 or 410. External links break more often than you expect.

  6. Thin pages

    "Coming soon" and near-empty pages still show up in search. Fill them in or remove them.

  7. Orphan pages

    Pages in the sitemap that nothing links to are rarely maintained. Link them from a relevant page, or retire them.

  8. Unique titles and headings

    Two pages titled "Getting started" confuse readers and search. Make each title say what is on the page.

  9. Ownership

    Every page that states a policy or limit needs a named owner and a review date.

  10. Redirects after restructuring

    When you move or rename pages, redirect the old addresses so links and bookmarks keep working.

How often to run it

Run the first five checks every time a policy, price or product name changes, and the whole list at least quarterly. Checks 1 to 7 can be automated: infoharness runs them in about two minutes, and the Pro plan repeats them on a schedule and emails you when something new appears.
