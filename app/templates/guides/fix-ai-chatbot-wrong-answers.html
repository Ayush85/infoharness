When an AI support bot answers wrong, the model is rarely the cause. It repeated what the documentation said, or one of several things the documentation said.

In short

  • Take a wrong answer, find the pages it came from, and read them side by side.
  • Four causes cover most cases: conflicting pages, stale pages, duplicates and missing pages.
  • Fix the source, then re-index. Prompt changes only hide the problem.

Trace one wrong answer

  1. Copy the question and the wrong answer.
  2. Search your help center for the key terms of the answer.
  3. Open every page that mentions it and compare what each one says.

The four usual causes

  • Conflicting pages. The refund policy says 14 days and the FAQ says 30. The bot answers with whichever it retrieves. Details: how contradictions become wrong answers.
  • Stale pages. An old guide describes a feature that changed. Nothing tells the bot it is old.
  • Duplicates. Two copies of a page, one updated. The bot may quote the old one.
  • Missing pages. No page answers the question, so the bot guesses.

Find them all, not one at a time

Tracing single answers finds single problems. A scan of the whole help center finds every conflict, duplicate and stale page at once, so you fix the causes of many wrong answers together. Check the documents your bot reads.
