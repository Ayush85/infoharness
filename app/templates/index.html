How it works

  1. Paste an address

    Your docs or help center URL. No account, no install, nothing to configure.

  2. We read the public pages

    The crawler follows your sitemap and links, obeys robots.txt and stays inside the docs folder you gave it.

  3. Fix what matters first

    You get a grade, every finding with quotes and page links, and a badge to show when it's clean.

Questions

Does it read private or logged-in pages?

No. It only reads pages anyone can open without signing in. Docs behind a login can't be scanned yet.

Will it work on my site?

It works on any docs or help center that serves its text as HTML, which covers most platforms. Sites that only draw their content with JavaScript show up as unreadable.

How are contradictions found?

By comparing the numbers, limits and dates that pages state about the same thing. Each finding shows both quotes side by side so you can judge it in seconds. Switching on AI verification also catches contradictions in wording.

Who can see my report?

Anyone who has the link. Reports are not listed anywhere and are hidden from search engines.

What does it cost?

Scanning is free. Pro watches your docs on a schedule and emails you when something new breaks.
