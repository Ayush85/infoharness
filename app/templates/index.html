    What every scan checks

    Seven checks, each with its own list of exact pages and quotes, so you know what to open and what to change.

    CheckWhat we flagLooks like
    ContradictionsDifferent prices, limits, deadlines or steps for the same fact on different pages.14 days on one page, 30 days on another
    Duplicate pagesTwo pages that say almost the same thing, so one will drift out of date.“Setup guide” and “Setup guide (copy)”
    Repeated sectionsThe same paragraph pasted into several articles.A warning copied into 12 pages
    Outdated contentPages that date themselves, or describe a version nobody else mentions.“Last updated: March 2019”
    Broken linksLinks inside your docs that return an error./docs/pricing → 404
    Thin pagesPlaceholders and near-empty pages that search still finds.“Coming soon.”
    Orphan pagesPages listed in your sitemap that no other page links to./docs/data-retention

    How it works

    1. Paste an address

      Your docs or help center URL. No account, no install, nothing to configure.

    2. We read the public pages

    3. Fix what matters first

      You get a grade, every finding with quotes and page links, and a badge to show when it's clean.
