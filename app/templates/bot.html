About the infoharness crawler

If you saw this in your server logs, someone asked infoharness to check the health of documentation on your site.

What it does

  • Reads up to {{ max_pages }} public pages under the URL it was given, plus your sitemap.
  • Obeys robots.txt. It never logs in, submits forms or runs JavaScript.
  • Checks links found on those pages with a single lightweight request each.

How to block it
