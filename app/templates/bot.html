About the infoharness crawler

If you found this in your server logs, someone asked infoharness to check the documentation on your site for contradictions and broken links.

What it does

  • Reads up to {{ max_pages }} public pages under the address it was given, plus your sitemap.
  • Obeys robots.txt. It never logs in, submits forms or runs JavaScript.
  • Checks each link it finds with one lightweight request.
  • Keeps a polite pace: a handful of requests at a time, with timeouts and size limits.

How it identifies itself

How to block it

Add this to your robots.txt and it will skip your whole site:

User-agent: InfoharnessBot
Disallow: /
